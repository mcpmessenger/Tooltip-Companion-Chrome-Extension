# Privacy Policy for Tooltip Companion

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2025

**Website:** [tooltipcompanion.com/privacy](https://tooltipcompanion.com/privacy)

> **Note:** This is a simplified version for the extension package. For the full, detailed Privacy Policy that will be hosted on tooltipcompanion.com, see `PRIVACY_POLICY_WEB.md`.

## Introduction

Tooltip Companion ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension and visit our website.

## Information We Collect

### Browser Extension

- **API Keys**: When you provide your OpenAI or other API keys, they are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's secure storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). We never send your API keys to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud synchronization via Google OAuth.
- **Page URLs**: The extension captures screenshots of external links you hover over. These screenshots are processed locally and cached in your browser's IndexedDB storage.
- **No Personal Data**: The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifying information about you.

### Website (tooltipcompanion.com)

When you visit our website and use Google OAuth with Supabase:
- **Authentication Information**: Your Google account information (name, email) for authentication purposes
- **API Keys** (optional): If you choose to sync your API keys via Google OAuth, they are stored securely in Supabase and encrypted at rest
- **Usage Analytics**: Basic analytics about website usage (page views, features used) through standard analytics tools

## How We Use Your Information

- **Local Storage**: All extension data (API keys, cached screenshots) is stored locally in your browser and never sent to external servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.
- **Cloud Sync** (Optional): If you enable Google OAuth on tooltipcompanion.com, your API keys are stored securely in Supabase for cross-device synchronization.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Google OAuth and Supabase

When you choose to enable cloud synchronization:
- We use Google OAuth for secure authentication
- Your API keys are stored in Supabase, which provides enterprise-grade encryption
- You can revoke access at any time through your Google account settings
- You can delete your data through the tooltipcompanion.com dashboard

## Data Security

- **Encryption**: API keys stored in Supabase are encrypted at rest
- **Local Storage**: Browser storage uses Chrome's built-in security mechanisms
- **HTTPS**: All communications with our servers are encrypted via HTTPS

## Your Rights

- **Access**: You can view your locally stored data in the extension's options page
- **Deletion**: You can delete your data at any time:
  - Local data: Clear browser storage or uninstall the extension
  - Cloud data: Delete through tooltipcompanion.com dashboard or contact support
- **Opt-Out**: You can use the extension without enabling cloud sync or providing API keys

## Third-Party Services

- **Supabase**: Used for cloud storage (only if you enable sync). See [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Google OAuth**: Used for authentication (only if you enable sync). See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI**: When you use the AI chat features, your queries are sent to OpenAI according to [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
